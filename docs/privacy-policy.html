Zoi Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Zoi (“Zoi,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a social experience-ranking app — you rank the things you do and share your taste with friends. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it’s shared, and the choices you have. By using Zoi, you agree to this policy.

Zoi is operated by Rushil Modali, an independent developer. If you have any questions, contact us at rushil.modali@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your phone number, which is used to sign you in with a one-time SMS code. You also provide a name and a @handle, and optionally a profile photo. Your name, handle, and profile photo are shown on your public profile.

Contacts (optional). If you choose to find friends already on Zoi, the app accesses your device contacts to check which of them use Zoi. We do not upload your contacts’ phone numbers. Each number is converted into an irreversible cryptographic hash (SHA-256) on your device, and only those hashes are sent to our server to look for matches. We do not store your contacts.

Location (optional). With your permission, Zoi uses your device location to help you find and tag places for your experiences and to display maps.

Photos (optional). If you add photos to an experience or set a profile picture, those images are uploaded to our storage and displayed in the app.

Content you create. The experiences you log, your rankings, trips and itineraries, notes, tags, saved (“wishlist”) items, and the accounts you follow.

Usage and device data. Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the app (such as app version, device type, and server logs) may be processed by our infrastructure providers.

How we use your information

  • Provide and operate the app — sign-in, ranking, feed, trips, and sharing.
  • Help you connect with friends — contact matching, suggestions, and follows.
  • Display your public taste profile and trips to other signed-in users (see “How information is shared”).
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and diagnose problems.
  • Communicate with you about your account, including sending your SMS login code.

How information is shared

With other users. Zoi is a social app built around sharing taste. Your profile (name, handle, photo), your ranked experiences, and your trips are visible to any signed-in Zoi user. Your wishlist (saved items) is private to you. Please don’t add content you wouldn’t want other users to see.

With service providers. We rely on trusted third parties to run Zoi:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, file storage, and server functions.
  • Twilio — delivers your SMS one-time login codes.
  • Google Places — powers place search and details. These requests are proxied through our server.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and security practices.

For legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and our service.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, experiences, trips, photos, and saved items. Hashed contact data is not retained after matching. Residual copies may persist briefly in routine backups or logs before being overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Permissions. Contacts, location, photos, and camera access are all optional and controlled in your device Settings — you can grant or revoke them at any time.
  • Edit your info. You can update your name, handle, and profile photo in the app.
  • Delete your account. You can request deletion of your account and its associated data by emailing us at rushil.modali@gmail.com, and we will remove it.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (such as access, correction, deletion, or portability). Contact us to exercise them.

Children

Zoi is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit, access controls, and on-device hashing of contact data. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the “Last updated” date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at rushil.modali@gmail.com.
